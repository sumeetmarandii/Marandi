Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with us

How long does it take to get a project quote?

+

We typically provide project quotes within 1-2 business days after our initial consultation. For complex projects, we may need 3-5 days to provide a detailed proposal.

Do you work with clients outside of New York?

+

Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide. Most of our communication happens via video calls, email, and project management tools. We've successfully completed projects for clients across the US and internationally.

What information should I include in my project inquiry?

+

Please include details about your project goals, target audience, timeline, budget range, and any specific features or requirements. The more information you provide, the better we can tailor our proposal to your needs.

Do you offer emergency support?

+

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency support for our maintenance clients. For urgent issues outside business hours, please call our emergency line or email us with \"URGENT\" in the subject line.
