Privacy policy

Marandi Internet respects your privacy. This website is a static informational site. It does not provide user accounts, does not include contact forms, and does not intentionally collect personal information through the site.

Cookies

The site does not set cookies for its own operation. Your browser, hosting provider, or external services may handle technical logs according to their own policies.
