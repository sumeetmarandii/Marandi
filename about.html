About Us

We design bold digital products that help brands grow, connect, and stand out with confidence.

Our Story

Marandi In. began with one clear belief: digital experiences should feel human, not generic. Since 2020, we have partnered with startups, local businesses, and growing brands to craft websites, interfaces, and campaigns that are visually sharp and performance-focused.

We combine strategic thinking, clean design systems, and modern development practices to transform ideas into products people trust. Every project is built to do more than look good. It should convert, communicate, and scale.
